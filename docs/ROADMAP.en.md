# Roadmap

[Português (Brasil)](ROADMAP.md) · [Español](ROADMAP.es.md)

## First release

- OpenCode, VS Code and Copilot CLI configuration through a picker or command-line parameters.
- Incremental additions, explicit replacement, removal based on availability and backups.
- Bundled catalogs, manual refresh and a monthly review workflow.
- OpenCode, FreeLLMAPI and endpoints defined through a manifest.
- Package testing and tag-based publishing with maintainer approval.

## Future work

- Monthly editorial review with PathBit's AI engine, including criteria and approval of changes. In the meantime, maintainers review the versioned selection against the monthly upstream patch.
- Additional client integrations and provider discovery from official catalogs.
